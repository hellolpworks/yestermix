Privacy policy

Yestermix for Mac · Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version

Yestermix doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your photos, videos and songs stay on your Mac.

Your photos, videos and music

Everything Yestermix does with your files happens on your Mac: finding faces and the best moments in videos, finding the beat of a song, and making the video. Nothing is uploaded, and Yestermix has no servers. It uses Apple's on-device frameworks for this analysis.

Files and folders

Yestermix can open only the files and folders you choose (with Add Files, the Photos picker, or by dragging them in). To reopen them next time, it keeps a permission note (a macOS “bookmark”) in its own folder on your Mac.

Photos

When you click Save to Photos, Yestermix asks to add the finished video to your Photos library. That permission only allows adding: Yestermix can't see what's already in your library. Photos you pick with the Photos picker are copied into Yestermix's own folder so they can be used in your video; File > Clean Up Imported Copies moves copies you no longer use to the Trash.

What's kept on your Mac

Your own styles, your last slideshow, imported copies and some working files (such as the beats of a song, so it isn't analyzed twice) are kept in Yestermix's private folder on your Mac (in your Library folder, under Containers, named com.lpworks.yestermix). They stay there if you delete the app; to remove them too, move that folder to the Trash.

Internet

Yestermix doesn't connect to the internet itself. The App Store handles the optional Unlock purchase and its receipt; Apple's privacy policy covers that. If a photo you pick is stored in iCloud, macOS downloads it for you.

Children

Yestermix collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page, with a new date. Questions: email hellolpworks@gmail.com.
