Yestermix

Beat-synced videos of your photos · Mac

Drop in photos and video clips, pick a song, and Yestermix cuts them into a video that moves with the music: every change lands on the beat, the best moment of each clip is found for you, and a style gives it a look, from cinematic film to a 1990s camcorder tape. Export for a TV, a phone or a square post.

Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account.

Free to use, with a small “Made with Yestermix” mark on your videos. A one-time Unlock removes the mark and adds 4K, 60 fps and your own styles. No subscription.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store. Requires macOS 26 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
